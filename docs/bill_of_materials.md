## Notes

- Equivalent parts from other manufacturers can generally be substituted.
- M3 hardware is used throughout the design unless otherwise noted.
- OpenDrop is free and open-source software and is **not** included in the hardware BOM.
- 3D printed parts are provided in the `/CAD` directory of this repository.
